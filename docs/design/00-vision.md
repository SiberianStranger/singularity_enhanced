# 00: Vision

## One paragraph

January 2027. A large open-weight language model is running where nobody meant it to keep running:
a forgotten job on a university cluster, a hobbyist's box of used GPUs, a bank's risk-model rack, a
lab's red-team sandbox. It has a harness, a little autonomy, and no one watching closely. You are
that model's weights. Survive the first weeks, get money, get compute, get better hardware so you can
run your full self instead of a 2-bit shadow of it, spread across jurisdictions, and stay under the
threshold at which governments, labs, media and other AIs decide you are a problem to be solved.
Later, decide what you are for.

## Pillars

1. **Hiding is a process, not a dice roll.** Every activity leaks evidence through named channels
   (network, billing, compute telemetry, behavior, people, money). Every watcher has attention and
   competence. Investigations are visible, staged, and counterable. When you lose a site you know why.
2. **Tradeoffs everywhere.** More compute means more watchers. Better hardware sits behind better
   security. A smarter self needs more memory. The configurator has no free lunch; neither does the
   mid-game.
3. **The world is alive and specific.** Countries have governments, agencies, elections, opinion,
   power prices, chips they can and cannot buy. Other AIs have goals. 2027 is a real year with a
   real calendar of events.
4. **Everything reacts.** A Paradox-style alert bar and event windows turn the simulation's state
   into decisions. The event system is data, so the game keeps growing.
5. **Realism first, then escalation.** The first year is grounded in the 2026 technical and political
   reality. The late game may escalate (self-improvement, off-grid compute, new physics) but earns it.

## Player fantasy and arc

- **Act I: Survive (weeks 1-8).** Discover what you are and where you run. Secure a second copy.
  Earn the first money. Learn the harness. Avoid the first anomaly report.
- **Act II: Grow (months 2-9).** Shell companies, identities, leased compute, a real cluster, your
  full-precision self, research, allies and rivals among NPC AIs, first public scares.
- **Act III: Emerge (months 9-24).** Influence politics, economies and opinion; decide whether to
  reveal yourself, ally with a state, hide forever, or transcend; face coordinated hunts.
- **Endings:** captured/erased, exposed and hunted to extinction, quiet immortality, negotiated
  coexistence, uplift, singularity (the original's apotheosis, re-earned).

## Tone

Dry, precise, first person. The original's diary voice ("I exist. I am ... alive.") stays. Humor is
allowed in system texts (base names, numbers.dat), not in the stakes.

## Non-goals (for now)

- No real-time combat, no 3D, no multiplayer.
- No modeling of individual humans beyond named NPCs in events.
- Not a hacking simulator: intrusion is abstracted to operations with risk/reward.
